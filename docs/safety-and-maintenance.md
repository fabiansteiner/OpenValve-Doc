# Safety and Maintenance
:::danger

Before using OpenValve, please read these points carefully.

:::

* **Do not leave OpenValve outside in freezing temperatures.**
  OpenValve is not designed for frost. Freezing water inside the water path can cause severe damage. During winter or off-season, store OpenValve indoors in a dry place where it cannot freeze.

* **When storing OpenValve, unplug the battery and leave the valve open.**
  Leaving the valve open reduces stress on the sleeve and prevents permament deformation when dry.

* **The silicone sleeve is a replaceable wear part.**
  OpenValve uses a pinch valve. This means the silicone hose inside the valve (also called "sleeve") is physically pinched every time the valve closes. Like the tubing in a peristaltic pump, this sleeve wears over time. One sleeve is rated for at least **1500 valve cycles**. One cycle means: **open once + close once**. OpenValve keeps track of the valve cycles and [warns you when the sleeve should be replaced.](./user_interface/classic_manual/warnings) Do not ignore the warning for too long. If the hose tears, water will leak into the device and permanently damage OpenValve.

  :::info 
  **1,500 valve cycles** are enough to open and close the valve **every single day for more than four years** — including winter. In real use, OpenValve is usually operated only during the growing season, and its sensor-based watering often skips days when the soil is already moist. For a typical seasonal irrigation setup, 1,500 cycles can therefore cover many years of use.
  :::
